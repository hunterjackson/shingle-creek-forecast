Track record

Every forecast is logged when it's issued and scored once the day has passed. The comparison is with the National Water Model, NOAA's nationwide forecast, which publishes day-1 flows for this creek.

Raw data

The same files this page reads, refreshed every few hours. Stages are in feet (NGVD29), rain in millimetres, times in UTC.

  • forecast.json — current reading and the 7-day forecast (expected level, likely range, flood category, rain)
  • observed.json — daily creek and Lake Toho levels for 45 days, hourly creek levels for 10 days
  • scorecard.json — verification of past forecasts
  • forecast_log.csv — every forecast issued, one row per day ahead

Experimental, independent forecast, not an official one. For flood warnings, follow the National Weather Service in Melbourne and Osceola County Emergency Management.
